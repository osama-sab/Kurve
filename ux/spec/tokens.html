Kurve token sheet

Phase 3 of the UX rework. Type, spacing and radii are proposed; the colour roles are Kurve's tokens as they are, plus a disabled ink and a pressed fill. Every ratio below is computed (tools/ux/tokens.py); the full list is ux/spec/contrast.md. 120 pairs, 0 below threshold.

Data palette: eight hues, in worksheet order

Kurve's "Distinct hues" scheme, with its light violet lightened to #6250d6, as the default. Validated with the six checks (lightness band, chroma, adjacent-pair colour-blind separation, normal-vision floor, contrast): it passes on the graph's light (#FFFFFF) and dark (#151C27) backgrounds. Three light slots are below 3:1 on white, so a legend is always shown for two curves or more. The current default fails both themes (see the spec).
