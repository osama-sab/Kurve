# Kurve's menu bar after the rework: every item.
#
# Two spaces per level. "  [Ctrl+O]" after a label is its shortcut. After " ## " a note:
#   ~ "old"     renamed from "old"
#   ← where     moved here from there
#   + what      another entry merged into this one
#   ↓ when      hidden by default: shown only when it can apply, or under More
#   new route   an existing command reached from here too (it was in a context menu)
# Lines starting "(…)" stand for a list the app fills in (graphs, fonts, sizes).
# tools/ux/menudiff.py checks that every item of today's menu bar is here or has a fate.
